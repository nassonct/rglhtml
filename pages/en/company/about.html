About

Who we are

Raphael Group Limited is a Tanzanian agribusiness group based in Mbeya, spanning grain processing, agricultural products, trading and distribution, and fuel retail.

Company overview

Transforming Agriculture Into Opportunity.

Raphael Group Limited is a Tanzanian company based in Mbeya. Our work spans agricultural sourcing, grain processing and value addition, agricultural products, trading and distribution, and fuel retail.

We operate across the value chain rather than at a single point in it. Produce is sourced from farming communities, processed and graded in our own facilities, then distributed to wholesale, retail and institutional buyers.

Our story

Established in the 2010s, Raphael Group grew from agricultural trade in the Mbeya region, where the southern highlands produce rice, maize, beans and groundnuts.

Building on this agricultural base, the company expanded its work across produce sourcing, grain processing and value addition, and distribution to wholesale, retail and institutional buyers. Today, these activities connect farming communities with markets across the value chain.

Our vision

To be a trusted Tanzanian agribusiness group that turns agricultural production into durable, shared economic opportunity.

Our mission

To source responsibly from farming communities, add value through disciplined processing, and supply markets with consistent, quality agricultural products.
