For farmers

Sell your produce to Raphael Group

Farmers and farmer groups can sell rice, maize, beans, groundnuts and sunflower to Raphael Group Limited. Submit your crop, location and estimated quantity.

A transparent process

Five steps from harvest to payment discussion

01

Grow

Harvest your crop and prepare it for sale — cleaned, dried and bagged where possible.

02

Share your location

Tell us your region and district so we can match you with the nearest collection point.

03

State your quantity

An estimate is enough — bags or tonnes. Both individual farmers and groups are welcome.

04

Submit the form

Send the enquiry below with a phone number or email we can reach you on.

05

Follow-up

Our sourcing team contacts you to confirm quality, terms and next steps.
