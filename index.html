Our value chain

From farmers, through processing, to markets.

01

Farmers

Produce is sourced from farming households and groups in producing districts.

02

Aggregation

Deliveries are received, weighed and prepared for processing or storage.

03

Processing

Cleaning, milling, grading and packing under one documented quality process.

04

Distribution

Warehousing and transport move product to buyers on agreed schedules.

05

Markets

Wholesale, retail, institutional and export buyers receive graded commodities.

About

A Tanzanian agribusiness group built around the crop.

Raphael Group Limited is a Tanzanian company based in Mbeya. Our work spans agricultural sourcing, grain processing and value addition, agricultural products, trading and distribution, and fuel retail.

We operate across the value chain rather than at a single point in it. Produce is sourced from farming communities, processed and graded in our own facilities, then distributed to wholesale, retail and institutional buyers.
